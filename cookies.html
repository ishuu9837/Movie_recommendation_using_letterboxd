Cookie & storage policy

Updated 11 October 2026 · Operator: MRS by Letterboxd

PrivacyCookiesTerms

MRS uses browser storage to respect your privacy choice and, when allowed, remember your cinema. There are no app-added advertising or analytics cookies.

Essential storage

KeyPurposeLifetime
mrs-consent-v1Your optional storage choiceUntil changed or cleared
mrs-enteredEntry state in this tabTab session
mrs-demo-session-v1Demo option and session state, not authenticationTab session

Optional preference storage

KeyPurpose
mrs-v5-profileFavourite, saved and watched film IDs
mrs-v5-preferencesLanguages, genres, exclusions, mood, runtime, rating and motion choices
mrs-v5-themeDark, light or device appearance

These keys are only written when optional storage is allowed. They remain until you clear them or withdraw permission. Earlier saved profile keys may be read to keep matching films; all legacy MRS profile keys are removed when you turn optional storage off.

Your controls

Enter the cinema and demo sign-in do not consent to optional storage. Choose Essential only, allow optional preferences, or use Cookie settings to change your choice. You can always save films and tune recommendations for the current visit. If storage is blocked or full, choices work in memory and the app reports the limit.

Hosting and external requests

The hosting platform may use its own technical cookies or storage for access and delivery. Its sign-in is separate from the demo card. External image hosts receive requests for posters; following Gmail or Letterboxd links is subject to those services’ policies.

Privacy policy

Updated 11 October 2026 · Operator: MRS by Letterboxd

PrivacyCookiesTerms

MRS by Letterboxd is an independent film discovery project. Contact: founder@ishuu.me.

Your cinema stays with you

Searches, filters, languages, genres, favourites, watchlist and watched status are processed in your browser. The app does not maintain an MRS server-side profile, sell your film choices, or sync them to a Letterboxd account. It adds no advertising or analytics scripts.

Demo sign-in

The sign-in card is a demo under development. Google, Apple, email and guest options all enter the same demo experience. No password, email address, provider account or OAuth connection is requested. A session marker remembers the selected demo option for this tab. It is not real authentication and does not protect or create an account.

Remembering preferences

Optional storage is off until you allow it. When enabled, localStorage keeps cinema preferences, appearance, favourites, watchlist and watched history on this browser. With essential-only storage, these choices work for the current visit and are not saved between visits. Your privacy choice is essential storage. Entry and the demo-session marker use sessionStorage.

Turning optional storage off removes saved film preferences, including earlier MRS profile keys. Use Preferences to export your choices or clear film data. Data belongs to this browser and website origin; it does not automatically transfer to another domain or device.

Technical requests and external services

The hosting provider receives technical request data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL and access time to deliver and secure the site. A private hosted version may require platform sign-in separately from MRS’s demo. MRS does not receive the hosting platform’s password.

Some posters load from a.ltrbxd.com. The image host receives your IP address and browser information. Poster requests use a no-referrer policy. Fonts, icons and bundled images are hosted with the site.

The contact card opens a Gmail compose page addressed to founder@ishuu.me. Opening it does not send an email. Gmail and Letterboxd links lead to external services with their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Saved film choices remain until you delete them, withdraw permission, or clear browser storage. The demo marker and entry state last for this tab’s session. The essential privacy choice stays until you change or clear it. There is no separate MRS server copy of your preferences.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact founder@ishuu.me. Rights and complaint routes depend on the laws that apply to you. We update this notice when the site’s behaviour changes.
